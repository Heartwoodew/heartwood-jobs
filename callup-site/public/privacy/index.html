Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

CallUp is operated by Heartwood Earthworx Pty Ltd (ABN 31 667 738 098), Brisbane, Queensland ("we", "us"). Contact: admin@callupapp.com.au.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send us: name, business, email, mobile.
  • Account and staff details entered into CallUp.
  • Business content you add: jobs, run sheets, photos, timesheets, leave, prestarts, SWMS, fleet records, reimbursements and team chat.
  • Basic technical info such as device and browser type and log data.

Why we use it

To provide and support CallUp, set up trials and accounts, bill subscriptions, keep the service secure and contact you about your account. We don't sell your information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that host and run CallUp, and anyone we're required to by law. We use Google Firebase / Google Cloud (app hosting, database and sign-in), Stripe (payments), Firebase Hosting (this website) and Forward Email (email forwarding). These providers may store data in Australia or overseas, including the United States.

Your business's data

Your business owns the content it puts into CallUp. Admins control which staff can see it.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information and keep it only as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations. After you cancel, your data is kept for 90 days and then deleted. You can export it before then.

Access and complaints

You can ask to access or correct your information, or complain, by emailing admin@callupapp.com.au. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
